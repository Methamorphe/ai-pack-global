Review the actual rendered result.

Inspect hierarchy, alignment, spacing, typography, overflow/clipping, density, contrast, whitespace, interaction states, responsive behavior, loading/empty/error states, and touch targets.

Classify issues as blocking, high impact, or polish. Return exact corrections and the three highest-impact improvements.
